The popup, dissected

🚩 Red flags in the popup you just saw

▸ Function was approve, not transfer. You expected to receive money. Why is the wallet asking permission to spend YOUR money?
▸ Amount was 115,792,089,... (uint256 max). That's "unlimited." No legit operation needs unlimited approval — exact amount is always enough.
▸ Spender is an unknown contract. Real protocols (SunSwap, JustLend) have verified, audited contracts you can look up. A fresh contract with no history is suspect.
▸ The pitch is "free money." No legitimate protocol gives 500 USDT for clicking a button. If it sounds too good to be true, the catch is in the popup.

The 4 things to always check before signing

Use this checklist on EVERY wallet popup, every time

1. Function name. If it says approve and you didn't expect to give permission to a contract → reject.

2. Amount. Should match exactly what you typed. If it's a giant number like 999,999,999 or 115,792,089,237,316,... → reject.

3. Spender / target address. Should match the dApp you're using. Cross-check on TronScan. If it's a fresh contract with no transactions → reject.

4. Your gut. If the dApp promised free money, said you need to "unlock" or "verify" or "initialize" before sending → reject and close the tab.

If you've already signed a bad approval

It can still be fixed — but you must act fast

1. Move funds out NOW. Open TronLink, send all your USDT to a brand-new wallet you control. The scammer's approval is to your OLD wallet — your new wallet has no approval and is safe.

2. Revoke the approval. On the old wallet, visit tronscan.org/#/tools/approvalCheck, paste your address, find the malicious contract in the list, click revoke. Costs ~1-2 TRX in gas.

3. Stop using the old wallet for anything important. Treat it as compromised. Any USDT that ever lands in it again is still at risk if you haven't revoked.
